## Overview
We are building a smart **Library Seat Management System** for our college library. The goal is to create a system that provides **real-time visibility and controlled management of library seating**, making seat usage fair and efficient.

Students currently have to physically roam around the library to find an empty seat. They often leave for short breaks (lunch, calls) while holding seats, effectively blocking them. Our system aims to solve this by bringing real-time status and temporary away permits.

## Core Features
1. **Real-Time Interactive Floor Map**: View live status of each seat across different floors and zones.
2. **Temporary Away / Break Permit**: A mechanism allowing students to step away (e.g. for 30 minutes) without losing their seat, while preventing indefinite blocking.
3. **Smart Seat Finder & Group Seating**: Get recommendations based on preferences like "quiet area," "power outlet," or find adjacent seats for a group.
4. **Fair Use & No-Show Protection**: Automatic seat release if students fail to check-in or exceed their break duration.
5. **Admin / Librarian Dashboard**: Live overview, seat management, configuration of break policies, and analytics.
